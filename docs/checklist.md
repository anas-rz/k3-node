## Notes on non-goals / caveats

- `k3_node.layers.kge.KGTripletLoader` replaces PyG's
  `torch.utils.data.DataLoader`-based loader with a framework-agnostic
  batching iterator (numpy-backed), since a torch-specific `DataLoader`
  doesn't fit a multi-backend (TF/PyTorch/JAX) library.
- `k3_node.layers.unpool.knn_interpolate` reuses `k3_node.layers.pool.knn`
  (a self-contained numpy brute-force k-NN) rather than PyG's `pyg-lib`/
  `torch-cluster`-backed extension.
- Methods that are inherently framework-agnostic-unfriendly (e.g. PyTorch's
  `torch.utils.checkpoint`, TorchScript export, Captum integration) are
  ported as no-ops or omitted, with notes above.
